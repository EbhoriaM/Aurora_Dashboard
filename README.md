# Aurora Retail & Digital Services Ltd. — Executive Business Intelligence Dashboard

### 📌  Project Overview

Aurora Retail & Digital Services Ltd. faces several business challenges, including profit volatility, missed revenue targets, and emerging customer churn. Traditional reporting methods often rely on static reports and visualizations, making it difficult for decision-makers to identify business risks and respond to performance changes promptly.

This project focuses on developing an executive-level Business Intelligence (BI) dashboard that combines key business performance indicators with AI-generated, business-friendly explanations of performance.

The solution is designed to transform business data into actionable insights, helping executives understand what is happening, why it matters, and what actions they should consider taking.

### 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objective is to build an interactive executive dashboard that goes beyond basic data visualization by combining performance monitoring, analytical insights, and actionable business recommendations.

The dashboard aims to:

Monitor revenue and profitability performance.

Compare actual revenue against budget targets.

Evaluate business performance across regions and sales channels.

Identify customer churn indicators and potential retention risks.

Automatically generate executive-ready summaries of business performance.

Highlight key risks, opportunities, and recommended management actions.

Support faster, evidence-based business decision-making.

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Power BI — Interactive dashboard development and KPI visualization.

SQL — Data querying, analysis, and preparation, where applicable.

Microsoft Fabric — Data preparation and pipeline development, where applicable.

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

#### The dashboard is designed to monitor the following business metrics:

KPI

Purpose

Total Revenue

Measures the total income generated from business activities.

Total Profit

Evaluates the company's profitability.

Revenue vs Budget

Compares actual revenue with budgeted revenue to identify performance gaps.

Regional Performance

Evaluates revenue and profitability across different regions.

Channel Performance

Identifies the sales channels contributing most to revenue and profit.

Customer Churn Indicators

Monitors customer losses and potential retention risks.
